# Fluids and Chemicals (M06)

Design and status of the chemistry/fluid foundation. Base: Mekanism 26.3 (`31bb4906`).

## Problem

Mekanism 26.3 has its own chemical registry (datapack `data/mekanism/mekanism/chemical/*.json`,
serializers `basic` and `enum_color_pigment`) and real world fluids (`MekanismFluids`). Horizon offers
content mods neither a chemical registry nor their own world fluids or buckets. Block entities can
have fluid tanks, though (API 54, hook `block_entity_fluids`). These tanks store **any fluid ID outside `minecraft`**,
save it in NBT (`fluids`) and move it with `fluid_insert` / `fluid_extract` / `move_fluids`
(API 54/55, `mod_fluids`).

## Decision

Both are tank IDs, with tables in `native/mekanism_native/api/src/chemicals_table.rs`
(generated by `scripts/gen_chemicals.py` from the upstream checkout: 18 fluids, 67 chemicals).

| Kind | Tank ID | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Fluid | `mekanism:<id>` (+ `minecraft:water`/`lava`) | `mekanism:brine` |
| Chemical | `mekanism:chemical/<id>` | `mekanism:chemical/hydrogen` |

- Liquid hydrogen (fluid) and hydrogen (chemical) never collide this way.
- `block_entity_accepts_fluid` separates the families: fluid tanks do not accept chemicals, chemical tanks
  do not accept fluids.
- No own network/datapack sync needed: Horizon syncs block entity NBT and menu data itself.
  The menu sends `chemicals::index + 1` (16 bit). Names and colors are in the native mod's client code.
- Unknown IDs (other mod, removed chemical) are kept and show their ID.

## Limits (Horizon)

- No world fluid blocks and no buckets for Mekanism fluids. Only water/lava buckets work on fluid tanks.
- Amounts are `i32` millibuckets. Creative tanks (Long.MAX_VALUE) hold `i32::MAX`.
- Pipe/tube networks (`native_move_fluids` only knows neighbor blocks) stay open, see group 2.

## Tank status (branch `feat/m06-chemistry-foundation`)

Fluid Tank and Chemical Tank, each Basic/Advanced/Elite/Ultimate/Creative: values from `FluidTankTier` /
`ChemicalTankTier`, auto-eject downward (fluid), dump modes `Idle`/`Dumping Excess`/`Dumping`
(chemical, `ChemicalUtils.dump`), contents kept on the item when broken, GUI with fill level and mode button,
debug block `mekanism:debug_chemical_source` (fills hydrogen into neighboring tanks).

Not ported: item slots for filling/emptying, side configuration, redstone, valve journal, edit mode.

**In-game tests and the native build (launcher) are still pending.** Unit tests: `cargo test --manifest-path logic/energy/Cargo.toml`.

## Electrolytic Separator and Rotary Condensentrator

Both run on the same tank table (`machine::FluidTanks`: gauge and capacity per tank, plus the tanks that can be filled and
drained) and `machine::convert` (one input, all outputs fit completely or nothing runs).
- Separator (`mekanism:separating`): tank 0 fluid input (24,000 mB), tank 1/2 left/right chemical (2,400 mB), one
  pass per tick. The importer turns the fluid tag into the fluid ID (`#c:brine` to `mekanism:brine`,
  `#minecraft:water` to `minecraft:water`) and skips the deuterium recipe (no tank ID for `mekanismgenerators` chemicals).
- Rotary (`mekanism:rotary`): tank 0 chemical, tank 1 fluid (10,000 mB each), mode in NBT `mekanism_rotary_mode`
  (0 chemical to fluid, 1 the reverse), button in the GUI (`BUTTON_MODE`). The menu appends the mode after the tanks.
Blocker: `block_entity_accepts_fluid` only knows block, tank number and fluid (no position, no mode), and
tank sides are static. So the Rotary mode cannot block input/output from outside. Fluid items and
chemical items (slots of the originals) are missing: Horizon has a fluid item API only for items, not for slot filling.

## Chemical output of machines (Chemical Oxidizer, Chemical Infuser)

The tanks of these machines are Horizon block entity tanks (`fluids`, order: inputs, then output). They are reachable from outside with
`block_entity_fluids`: inputs can be filled, output can be drained. Chemical recipes: `oxidizing`
(item to chemical, `Recipe::chemical_output`) and `chemical_infusing` (`recipe::Infusion`, both sides
swappable). The output is pushed every tick (up to 1,024 mB) with `fluid_insert` into all six neighbors
(Mekanism's ejector with side config is not ported). `block_entity_accepts_fluid` gets no position and
no tank contents. So the Infuser accepts any chemical that appears in a recipe. If the partner
does not fit, the machine waits.

Blockers/limits: no item slot fill of the tanks (chemical items are missing), amounts `i32`, no arrow overlay.
The eject call is outside the recipe lock, because `fluid_insert` calls the neighbor's `accepts` hook
synchronously (same mutex: otherwise deadlock).

## Electric Pump and Fluidic Plenisher

Logic in `pump.rs` (Horizon-free, tests in `logic/energy`), host side in `pumps.rs`. Both have a 10,000 mB tank as a Horizon block entity tank (`minecraft:water` / `minecraft:lava`), 2 FE/t, 8 FE storage.

- **Pump:** one source every 19 ticks (1,000 mB), first below the pump, then the neighbors of already pumped blocks within radius 80 (recursive, like `TileEntityElectricPump`). The tank outputs up to 256 mB/t upward (`fluid_insert`). Water with game rule `water_source_conversion` and lava with `lava_source_conversion` do not disappear (Mekanism's `pumpInfiniteFluidSources` is off).
- **Plenisher:** takes water/lava only from above. One block every 20 ticks: first the breadth-first search over air and flowing fluid below (never upward, at most 4,000 blocks), then it places one bucket per pass. The path is stored in the block entity NBT (`mekanism_plenisher_*`).
- World access: `Host::block`, `block_property` (`level` 0 = source), `set_block` (flags 3), `Level::is_loaded` and `Level::game_rule`. Unloaded chunks are never read or written.
## Ore processing (Dissolution Chamber, Chemical Washer, Chemical Crystallizer)

Slurries (`dirty_*`, `clean_*`) are chemicals of the table (`mekanism:chemical/dirty_iron`). Dissolution: item + acid
from tank 0 (`Machine::chemical_tank`, per-tick consumption) to slurry in tank 1. Washer: `machine::wash` (water + slurry
to slurry, all or nothing). Crystallizer: `Op::Crystallize`, chemical from tank 0 to item in the output slot. Recipe shapes
`dissolution`, `washing`, `crystallizing` in `recipe.rs`. Overview and limits: `docs/ORE_PROCESSING.md`.

## Nutritional Liquifier, Pigment Extractor, Pigment Mixer, Painting Machine

No new operation: the Liquifier and the Extractor are `Op::Item` like the Oxidizer, with chemical/fluid output (`Recipe::chemical_output`; `liquification` and `pigment_extracting` have the same shape as `oxidizing`). The Mixer is `Op::Infuse` with its own recipe type (`pigment_mixing`; `Recipes::infusing` keeps the table per recipe type, so Infuser and Mixer do not displace each other). The Painting Machine is `Op::Item` like the Dissolution Chamber, with chemical from an input tank (once at the end, `per_tick_usage` false). Paste is a fluid (`mekanism:nutritional_paste`), pigments are chemicals. For IDs that exist only as a fluid, `chemicals::from_recipe` returns the fluid tank ID.

Values (`machine.rs`, Mekanism 26.3): Liquifier 8 FE/t, 100 ticks, tank 10,000 mB; Extractor 5 FE/t, 100 ticks, 20,000 mB; Mixer 5 FE/t, one pass per tick, inputs 1,000 mB each, output 2,000 mB; Painting 5 FE/t, 200 ticks, 15,000 mB.

Limits: the Liquifier only knows the vanilla foods from `gen_resources.py` (`FOODS`). Horizon's native API does not read an item's food values, so Mekanism's dynamic recipe (`createDynamicRecipe`, food x 50 mB) cannot be rebuilt. The return items (bowl, bottle) and the container slot are missing. Pigment items (chemical item slots of the originals), energy slot, upgrades, side config and redstone are not ported. The Pigment Mixer is not craftable until `hdpe_rod` (HDPE, Pressurized Reaction Chamber) exists.

## Mechanical Pipes, Pressurized Tubes, Thermodynamic Conductors (group 2)

Twelve blocks (four tiers each), values from `PipeTier`, `TubeTier`, `ConductorTier` (Mekanism 26.3), logic Horizon-free in `transmitter.rs` (tests in `logic/energy`), host in `transmitters.rs`.

| Tier | Pipe (fluid) capacity / rate | Tube (chemical) capacity / rate | Conductor insulation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | 2,000 / 250 mB/t | 4,000 / 750 mB/t | 10 |
| Advanced | 8,000 / 1,000 | 16,000 / 2,000 | 400 |
| Elite | 32,000 / 8,000 | 256,000 / 64,000 | 8,000 |
| Ultimate | 128,000 / 32,000 | 1,024,000 / 256,000 | 100,000 |

Horizon has no transmitter networks, so each block is its own block entity:
- **Pipe/Tube**: one block entity tank (`block_entity_fluids`, input only; `block_entity_accepts_fluid` separates fluids and chemicals). Every tick the block offers liquid to its neighbors, up to the tier's rate, and only to those with room and a lower fill level than its own (`push_plan`). A line balances itself this way, and nothing flows back into a fuller tank. Sources push into the pipe themselves, like tanks, machine outputs and pumps. Difference from Mekanism: capacity and rate apply per block, not for the whole network.
- **Conductor**: heat in `mekanism_heat` (like the Heat Generator). Per tick, `ITileHeatHandler.simulateAdjacent` to colder neighbor sides (conductors, Heat Generator) and `simulateEnvironment` (six sides, air 10,000 + insulation + conduction 5). Newly placed conductors start at the biome's ambient temperature. The Heat Generator gives heat to a colder conductor and takes it from a warmer one.
- **Connections**: block states `down`..`east` (boolean), set by the tick: a transmitter of the same kind of any tier, a block with a fluid tank (Pipe/Tube) or a Heat Generator (Conductor). The model is a multipart combination of a center cube and six arms (cuboids with the upstream textures), because Horizon does not know Mekanism's OBJ loader.

Not ported: side modes Pull/Push/Off (Configurator), network redstone, upgrade by tier installer, a selection box per arm (Horizon shapes depend on one property), heat sources other than the Heat Generator (Boiler, Fission, Fusion, Resistive Heater do not exist yet). Limit: `block_entity_accepts_fluid` knows no position, so a pipe cannot block by neighbor.

## M06 Pressurized Reaction Chamber, Isotopic Centrifuge, Solar Neutron Activator (13.11.2026, branch `feat/m06-advanced-chem`)

All three run on the shared machine framework (`machine::MACHINES`, `process::tick`, `recipe.rs`). The recipes (`mekanism:reaction`, `mekanism:centrifuging`, `mekanism:activating`) come from the upstream import. **Untested in-game**, test plan in [TESTLOG.md](TESTLOG.md).

| Machine | Values (source Mekanism 26.3) | Slots and tanks |
|---|---|---|
| Pressurized Reaction Chamber | `pressurizedReactionBase` 2 FE/t plus the recipe's `energy_required`, duration per recipe (`duration`), buffer 1,800 FE | Slot 0 input, slot 1 output; tanks: 0 fluid, 1 chemical (inputs), 2 chemical output, 10,000 mB each |
| Isotopic Centrifuge | 8 FE/t, storage 32 FE, one pass per tick | Tank 0 input, tank 1 output (10,000 mB) |
| Solar Neutron Activator | no energy; `maxSolarNeutronActivatorRate` 64 mB/t times biome peak factor times sun brightness | Tanks like the Centrifuge |

- **Reaction**: energy per tick `2 + energy_required`. Progress counts ticks up to `duration` (raw in NBT, as a percentage in the menu). At the end, item, fluid and chemical are consumed and item and/or chemical are produced, all or nothing: if an output does not fit, nothing runs (`machine::can_react`, `react`). The buffer is sized for the longest recipe (900 ticks at 2 FE/t) instead of growing per recipe.
- **Centrifuge**: one pass per tick, limited by input and room in the output (`machine::transmute`).
- **Activator**: rate as in `recalculateProductionRate` (biome peak through `generator::peak_multiplier`, brightness from time of day and weather, 0 without open sky). Below 1 mB/t, one pass every `ceil(1/rate)` ticks, otherwise `floor(rate)` passes per tick (`machine::activation_schedule`). The progress bar shows the sun's share of the peak rate. The tooltip shows "Production" and "Peak".
- **Chemicals**: `gen_chemicals.py` now also reads the Generators chemicals (`deuterium`, `fusion_fuel`, `tritium`; 67 instead of 64). `chemicals::from_recipe` resolves `mekanismgenerators:` IDs so that the Activator's tritium recipe runs.
- **Items**: `substrate` and `hdpe_pellet` are new (Reaction outputs). Importer changes: `neoforge:or` also for recipes with a fluid input, chemical tags in `input` (Centrifuge/Activator), a chemical output in the Reaction recipe does not count as an item.

Limits:
- **Second block**: in Mekanism, the Centrifuge (model 32 high) and the Activator (29 high) are two blocks high (`BlockBounding`). The bounding system here is generator-specific and was not carried over. The upper part of the model sticks out over the cube without its own block and can be clipped or wrongly culled. Check in-game.
- **Activator**: without energy and without sound (Mekanism has neither). It only works with open sky above the block (brightness test like the Solar Generator).
- **Reaction recipes** with plutonium/polonium pellets (`pellet_plutonium`, `pellet_polonium`) are missing, because the items (radiation system) are not registered. The `#c:rods/wooden` recipe is missing (tag does not exist). The deuterium Separator recipe stays skipped.
- Not ported, as with the other chemistry machines: energy slot, upgrades, side config, chemical item slots, redstone, arrow animation. The Reaction GUI is 176 x 166 (Mekanism: 171 high).
- Sound lengths (49 and 110 ticks) come from the imported `.ogg` files. They loop like the other machines.
